# Daily Portfolio Digest

## 1. Executive Summary
Several portfolio companies reported Q2 2026 earnings or key operational updates with generally constructive signals, though mixed sentiment emerged on a few names due to legal risks or cautious outlooks. Notably, Ouster delivered a 70% Y/Y jump in LiDAR shipments but faced a slight share pullback after mixed revenue guidance. ServiceNow’s expanded partnership with Tech Mahindra to scale enterprise AI drives positive outlook. Texas Instruments remains nuanced post-earnings amid price volatility despite fair value analyst boosts. Legal cloud lingers over Super Micro Computer despite cleared investigations.

## 2. Highest-Signal Portfolio Updates

- **Ouster (OUST)**: Q2 LiDAR shipments surged 70% Y/Y, indicating strong demand (Source: [OUST Earnings](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/articles/ousters-lidar-shipments-jump-70-160000872.html)). However, the stock declined 5.7% following a Utah lidar contract win and mixed Q3 revenue outlook, raising questions on demand sustainability.

- **ServiceNow (NOW)**: Announced expansion of partnership with Tech Mahindra to deliver production-ready enterprise AI at scale, reinforcing its AI automation and workflow software leadership (Source: [NOW Partnership](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/tech-mahindra-servicenow-expand-partnership-140000979.html)). Shares are up 41.1% since last earnings, reflecting strong market confidence.

- **Texas Instruments (TXN)**: Mixed earnings reception; top sector analyst coverage highlights strong Q2 operational metrics but the stock is down ~6.8% since earnings. Analysts have raised fair value targets, reflecting a nuanced but positive valuation environment (Sources: [TXN Highlights](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/analog-semiconductors-stocks-q2-highlights-173915571.html), [TXN Price Drop](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/why-texas-instruments-txn-down-153013726.html)).

- **CPS Technologies (CPSH)**: Reported record revenue and strategic progress for Q2, with generally constructive earnings coverage (Source: [CPSH Earnings](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/cps-technologies-corp-cpsh-q2-010513160.html)).

- **Amprius Technologies (AMPX)**: Delivered record revenue in Q2 and raised guidance, driven by increased drone demand—signal constructive for battery tech exposure (Source: [AMPX Earnings](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/amprius-technologies-inc-ampx-q2-210251235.html)).

- **Adeia (ADEA)**: Mixed reception to Q2 results; revenue hits $96M, but coverage is cautious despite new IP license agreements, suggesting moderate growth outlook (Source: [ADEA Earnings](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/adeia-inc-adea-q2-2026-050634400.html)).

- **Super Micro Computer (SMCI)**: Ongoing legal risk narratives cloud sentiment despite cleared senior management in export control investigations and no evidence of wrongdoing, with mixed views on valuation relative to AI growth prospects (Source: [SMCI Litigation](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/super-micro-computer-smci-fairly-161258897.html)).

- **Sivers Semiconductors (SIVE)**: Upcoming Q2 report presentation invites attention; recent $8.2M production order for Ka-Band beamforming ICs highlights commercial traction in optical/wireless semis (Source: [SIVE Presentation](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/invitation-presentation-sivers-semiconductors-q2-052500947.html)).

- **Vicor (VICR)**: Strong Q2 revenue growth and strategic initiatives noted (Source: [VICR Earnings](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/vicor-corp-vicr-q2-2026-190050344.html)).

- **Infineon Technologies (IFX)**: Set record AI revenue indicating increasing penetration in AI hardware markets; valuation questions remain (Source: [IFX AI Revenue](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/infineon-technologies-xtra-ifx-sets-151151454.html)).

- **Ouster (OUST)**: Despite strong shipment growth, mixed Q3 revenue outlook could signal demand variability, warranting monitoring.

- **Texas Instruments (TXN)**: Significant recent share price weakness post-earnings suggests underlying investor concerns or broader analog semiconductor market volatility.
