## 1. Executive Summary

Several portfolio companies reported recent earnings with constructive themes highlighted by record or beat-topline results and raised outlooks in key sectors: optical networking (AAOI), lidar/perception hardware (OUST), photonics/wireless semiconductors (SIVE), advanced materials (CPSH), battery technology (AMPX), and broadband/video infrastructure (HLIT). Enterprise AI and data center infrastructure saw notable product launches and partnerships: NOW’s AI-focused collaboration, PENG’s AI memory solutions, and AMPX’s outlook lift. Adeia (ADEA) was an outlier, lagging Q3 earnings and revenue expectations, warranting attention. No immediate risks surfaced except ongoing defense orders for CPSH. The mix of earnings, partnerships, and product innovations signals growth execution and secular AI/data infrastructure demand continuation.

---

## 2. Highest-Signal Portfolio Updates

- **OUST (Ouster) [Lidar]**: Recorded Q3 revenue hit a new peak, leading to raised guidance and constructive investor sentiment on valuation (Ref: Ouster record revenue, raised outlook).  
- **AAOI (Applied Optoelectronics) [Optical networking]**: Reported record Q3 revenue and returned to GAAP profitability driven by AI data center demand, fueling share price gains (Ref: AAOI record revenue, profit return).  
- **SIVE (Sivers Semiconductors) [Photonics, wireless]**: Q2 pipeline surged 268% exceeding $1B, with strategic global agreements and production order wins for LiDAR customers (Ref: Sivers strong pipeline, new orders).  
- **HLIT (Harmonic) [Broadband, video]**: Q3 earnings signaled record revenue surge and raised outlook; multiple corroborating reports highlight sustained momentum in broadband/video delivery (Ref: HLIT record revenue, raised outlook).  
- **CPSH (CPS Technologies) [Advanced materials]**: Posted record Q2 revenue and disclosed strategic defense tungsten components order, signaling growth diversification (Ref: CPSH record revenue, defense orders).  
- **AMPX (Amprius Technologies) [Battery tech]**: Beat Q3 revenue estimates despite a loss, and lifted its 2026 outlook on the back of high-energy-density battery demand (Ref: AMPX earnings beat, outlook lift).  
- **NOW (ServiceNow) [Enterprise AI]**: Partnership announcement on agentic AI integration touched off constructive price reaction reflecting traction in AI automation workflows (Ref: NOW AI partnership coverage).  
- **PENG (Penguin Solutions) [AI/HPC infrastructure]**: Multiple product developments around AI memory reuse, CXL-enabled DDR4 modules, and AI services underline pivot towards AI data center infrastructure growth (Ref: PENG AI memory innovations, new products).  

Less constructive:  
- **ADEA (Adeia) [IP licensing]**: Lagged on Q3 earnings and revenue estimates, warranting further scrutiny of business trajectory (Ref: Adeia earnings/revenue miss).
