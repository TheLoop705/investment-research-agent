# Daily Portfolio Digest

## 1. Executive Summary
Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing (TSM) and CPS Technologies (CPSH) reported earnings underscoring their strategic positioning in AI-related growth areas and industrial materials, respectively. ServiceNow (NOW) guidance and analyst optimism reflect continued confidence in AI-driven software adoption. Marvell Technology (MRVL) advanced its AI infrastructure role via Nvidia partnerships and acquisitions. Ouster (OUST) expanded a key lidar sensor production partnership, strengthening hardware scale potential. Cautious tones emerged from American Superconductor (AMSC) on earnings vs. growth outlook. Ambarella (AMBA) and Sivers Semiconductors (SIVE) showed steady progress aligned with automotive and photonics markets. Super Micro Computer (SMCI) garnered a positive upgrade amid growing AI infrastructure relevance. Several firms posted optimistic revenue growth forecasts despite profitability or valuation headwinds.

## 2. Highest-Signal Portfolio Updates
- **TSM**: Strong earnings demonstrate TSM’s pivotal role in the AI-capex cycle; price target raised on advanced node growth expectations. Despite some U.S. chip stock pressures, TSM anchors AI semiconductor supply chain growth.  
- **CPSH**: Record revenues in Q4 2025 drive bullish outlook for 2026 growth; recently priced a $9.6M at-the-market stock offering.  
- **NOW**: Oppenheimer reaffirms bullish rating fueled by robust AI growth and a new Cognizant partnership; software sector rotation benefits NOW.  
- **MRVL**: Aggressive AI expansion via Nvidia collaboration and dual acquisitions broadens product and market footprint.  
- **OUST**: Partnership with Benchmark expanded to scale volume production of REV8 digital lidar sensors; also aligning lidar to perception platforms for AI-powered machinery.  
- **AMBA**: Q1 earnings met estimates with automotive segment strength, signaling steady semiconductor demand growth in auto vision.  
- **SIVE**: Reported strategic growth and solid Q1 2026 results consistent with photonics and wireless semiconductor demand.  
- **SMCI**: GF Securities upgraded SMCI stock amidst increasing lunar/space AI communications contracts and infrastructure relevance.  
- **LPK**: Revenue growth forecasts support optimism despite concerns on profitability margins after Q4 earnings.  
- **AMSC**: Revenue growth remains robust, but earnings miss and cautious guidance introduce valuation uncertainties.

- **LPK**: Profitability concerns persist even as revenue forecasts are positive, highlighting margin pressures.  
- No explicit risks reported for TSM, NOW, MRVL, OUST, AMBA, SIVE, or SMCI at present.
