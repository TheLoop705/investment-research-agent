# Daily Portfolio Digest

## 1. Executive Summary
Key developments include several earnings results with mostly constructive outlooks (CPSH, LPK, PENG) and cautious notes (AMSC). ServiceNow (NOW) received renewed bullish guidance amid AI growth, while Taiwan Semiconductor (TSM) reported a significant AI packaging partnership. Ouster (OUST) expanded a key partnership to scale production of its new REV8 lidar sensors. Ambarella (AMBA) showed strong auto revenue but faces mixed regulatory sentiment. Overall, AI and industrial infrastructure remain dominant catalysts across the portfolio.
