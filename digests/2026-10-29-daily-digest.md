# Daily Portfolio Digest

## 1. Executive Summary
Key earnings reports dominated today’s portfolio developments with mixed sentiment: Texas Instruments (TXN) saw improving earnings estimates and constructive margin commentary, while ServiceNow (NOW) faced a cautious regulatory environment and a subscription sales outlook that fueled AI-related investor concerns. Ouster (OUST) and Super Micro Computer (SMCI) reported upcoming or recent earnings with mixed reactions.
