# Daily Portfolio Digest

## 1. Executive Summary
Several portfolio companies released earnings or other key updates, with predominantly positive signals across multiple sectors such as semiconductors, photonics, AI infrastructure, and energy materials. Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing (TSM) set a revenue record amid mixed investor sentiment related to executive changes and AI market concerns. Harmonic (HLIT), CPS Technologies (CPSH), ServiceNow (NOW), Ouster (OUST), Sivers Semiconductors (SIVE), Marvell (MRVL), and Applied Optoelectronics (AAOI) reported strong revenue growth and raised outlooks. Meanwhile, Amprius Technologies (AMPX) beat revenue estimates despite a loss, and Adeia (ADEA) lagged on earnings and revenue.

- **TSM (Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing)**
  - Hit record revenue, confirming continued demand "far above" supply.
  - Investor caution due to Jensen Huang’s exit from chip industry leadership and AI debt fears.
  - TSMC stock has risen 23% YTD but recently slipped as market rebalances (Sources: [TSM](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/taiwan-semiconductor-manufacturing-nyse-tsm-030943458.html?.tsrc=rss)).

- **CPSH (CPS Technologies)**

- **NOW (ServiceNow)**

- **SIVE (Sivers Semiconductors)**

  - Strategic agreement with WT Microelectronics extends global market reach.

- **MRVL (Marvell Technology)**
  - Added three new directors following AI memory solution reveal at SC26.
  - Collaboration with Penguin Solutions targets data center AI memory and inference acceleration.
  - Stock pressured by Nvidia-related financing pullback and AI connectivity concerns (Sources: [MRVL](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/marvell-technology-mrvl-adds-three-191207405.html?.tsrc=rss)).

- **AAOI (Applied Optoelectronics)**
  - Reported record revenue and return to growth in Q3 2026.
  - Stock up 7–12% post-earnings, supported by strong optical networking demand.
  - CoreWeave’s AI growth helping revive optics suppliers broadly (Sources: [AAOI](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/applied-optoelectronics-inc-aaoi-q3-111025689.html?.tsrc=rss)).

- **AMPX (Amprius Technologies)**
  - Q3 results showed revenue beats despite reporting a loss.
  - Company remains active in high-energy-density battery technology for aviation and energy-storage applications (Sources: [AMPX](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/amprius-technologies-ampx-reports-q3-214001660.html?.tsrc=rss)).

- **ADEA (Adeia)**
  - Missed Q3 earnings and revenue estimates.

## 3. Risk Flags

- **TSM** faces share price pressure linked to Nvidia leadership changes and market uncertainties around AI-related demand.
- **MRVL** stock declined by nearly 7% amid concerns about Nvidia-related financing and AI connectivity strategy.
- **OUST** must sustain revenue growth expectations to justify valuation — investor sentiment mixed between growth optimism and high valuation caution.
- **ADEA** missing estimates highlights potential volatility in IP licensing businesses sensitive to semiconductor cycle shifts.
- **TSM and Intel have broader sector risk**, as indicated by the related Intel ETF drop and shareholder lawsuits potentially affecting market perception.

## 4. Cross-Holding Themes

- **AI and Data Center Growth:** Marvell, ServiceNow, Penguin Solutions, Super Micro Computer, and Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing show connected momentum around AI infrastructure and acceleration solutions.
- **Photonics & Optical Networking:** Sivers Semiconductors, Applied Optoelectronics, and Nynomic reflect strong demand in photonics and lidar-related markets.
- **Energy & Materials:** CPS Technologies and Amprius Technologies continue to develop defense-grade materials and advanced battery technologies, respectively.
- **Semiconductor Supply Chain & Capital Equipment:** Taiwan Semiconductor, ASE Technology, X-FAB, Infineon, LPKF Laser, and Samsung Electronics highlight continuing complexity and opportunity in manufacturing and specialty foundries.
- **Valuation and Market Sentiment Sensitivity:** Some holdings, especially in high-growth AI chip and infrastructure sectors (TSM, MRVL, OUST), face near-term volatility amid changing market narratives.
