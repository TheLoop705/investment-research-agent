# Daily Portfolio Digest – June 2024

## 1. Executive Summary
Key earnings and partnership updates dominated today’s portfolio developments. Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing (TSM) and Texas Instruments (TXN) reported earnings with generally constructive outlooks fueled by strong mid-year revenue and data center demand. ServiceNow (NOW) expanded a strategic AI partnership with Tech Mahindra, reinforcing its enterprise AI growth trajectory. Ouster's (OUST) LiDAR shipments surged 70% year-over-year in Q2, indicating demand momentum despite mixed revenue outlooks. Super Micro Computer (SMCI) remains under investor scrutiny due to recent legal risks, clouding its AI-related growth narrative.

## 3. Risk Flags

- **SMCI**: Legal uncertainties including export control investigations remain a market sentiment drag, raising concerns on sustainable AI growth prospects.  
- **OUST**: Mixed Q3 revenue outlook despite shipment growth prompts caution. Contract wins may not yet fully offset near-term guidance concerns.  
- **ADEA (Adeia)**: Earnings show mixed results, signaling need for closer scrutiny on revenue trends and IP licensing impact.

## 4. Cross-Holding Themes

- **AI and Data Center Demand**: Strength across AI-related semiconductors and infrastructure firms (TSM, TXN, SMCI, NOW, MRVL) hinges on continued data center growth and enterprise AI adoption.  
- **Supply Chain Tightening & Pricing Power**: Foundry capacity (TSM, XFAB) tightening supports pricing but raises valuation debates on long-term growth sustainability.  
- **Growth vs. Regulatory/Legal Risks**: SMCI and OUST highlight tension between rapid growth in AI/autonomy markets and operational/legal uncertainties.  
- **Energy and Materials Resilience**: CPSH and AMPX showing robust Q2 performance amid broader clean energy and materials trends influencing energy storage and thermal management.

## 5. Questions For Follow-Up

- How will TSM’s capacity constraints influence semiconductor pricing and customer demand going into H2 2024?  
- What is the outlook for Ouster’s LiDAR demand beyond Q3 given mixed guidance and recent contract wins?  
- Can SMCI mitigate legal risks timely to maintain its AI hardware growth trajectory?  
- What are the implications of ServiceNow’s expanded partnership on competitive positioning against Palantir and Salesforce in AI SaaS?  
- How sustainable are the record revenue trends reported by CPSH and AMPX amid macroeconomic uncertainties?
