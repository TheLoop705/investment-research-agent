# Daily Portfolio Digest

## 1. Executive Summary
Recent earnings reports highlight strong revenue growth and improved outlooks across key holdings, with notable strength in semiconductors, AI infrastructure, and advanced materials. Marvell (MRVL) and TSMC (TSM) reported mixed earnings but reinforced their leading roles in AI and photonics. Battery maker Amprius (AMPX) delivered record revenues and raised guidance, while several technology-centric names such as Harmonic (HLIT), CPS Technologies (CPSH), and Sivers Semiconductors (SIVE) showed pipeline expansion and strategic progress. Ouster’s (OUST) lidar shipments surged 70% year-over-year, but sustainability of demand remains questioned.

## 2. Highest-Signal Portfolio Updates

- **Marvell Technology (MRVL)**  
  - Raised revenue outlook but reported lower free cash flow margins.  
  - Emphasis on pushing deeper into AI photonics, signaling strategic emphasis beyond traditional data center and networking markets.  
  - Analyst signals suggest MRVL might be undervalued by up to 25%.  
  ([Source](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/marvell-technology-mrvl-could-25-010808144.html?.tsrc=rss))

- **Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing (TSM)**  
  - Delivered stellar financial results, continuing to lead foundry capacity, especially in AI compute chips.  
  - Advancing A14 process technology roadmap to accelerate AI transformation.  
  ([Source](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/taiwan-semiconductor-manufacturing-company-tsm-131831930.html?.tsrc=rss))

- **Amprius Technologies (AMPX)**  
  - Reported record Q2 revenue and increased guidance, driven by demand in drone and energy-storage applications.  
  ([Source](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/amprius-technologies-inc-ampx-q2-210251235.html?.tsrc=rss))

- **Adeia (ADEA)**  
  - Q2 revenue reached $96 million, mixed earnings tone but with notable IP licensing progress, including multi-year agreements adding recurring revenue potential.  
  ([Source](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/adeia-inc-adea-q2-2026-050634400.html?.tsrc=rss))

- **CPS Technologies (CPSH)**  
  - Achieved record revenue and secured an initial defense order for tungsten-based thermal components, reinforcing strategic footprint in advanced materials & thermal management for defense.  
  ([Source](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/cps-technologies-corp-cpsh-q2-010513160.html?.tsrc=rss))

- **Sivers Semiconductors (SIVE)**  
  - Pipeline surged 268% to over $1 billion, underpinned by product growth and customer ramps in photonics and wireless semiconductors.  
  ([Source](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/sivers-semiconductors-ab-sivef-q2-010113113.html?.tsrc=rss))

- **Harmonic (HLIT)**  
  - Beat earnings estimates, raised guidance, and reported record backlog, driven by broadband infrastructure expansion and 54% increase in broadband segment revenue.  
  ([Source](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/harmonic-hlit-cheap-earnings-beat-161222966.html?.tsrc=rss))

- **Ouster (OUST)**  
  - LiDAR shipments jumped 70% Y/Y in Q2 amid growth in industrial autonomy (e.g., John Deere), yet sustainability of demand remains a key question.  
  ([Source](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/articles/ousters-lidar-shipments-jump-70-160000872.html?.tsrc=rss))

## 3. Risk Flags
- **OUST**: Uncertainty about whether current strong lidar shipment growth can be sustained long term raises demand risk.  
- **ADEA**: Mixed earnings suggest potential volatility linked to IP licensing revenue sustainability and dependency on few major agreements.  
- **MRVL**: Lower free cash flow margins despite revenue growth could pressure near-term cash generation; needs monitoring.

## 4. Cross-Holding Themes
- **AI & Photonics Expansion**: Both MRVL and TSM emphasize AI-focused technology development, with MRVL diverging deeper into AI photonics and TSM advancing AI-specific process nodes.  
- **Advanced Materials and Thermal Solutions**: CPSH’s defense order and SIVE’s pipeline growth highlight sector momentum in specialty wireless and photonics components linked to high-performance electronics.  
- **Energy Storage & Infrastructure Growth**: AMPX’s record revenue and raised guidance coincide with broader market themes in drone and high-energy-density battery tech.  
- **Networking & Broadband Infrastructure**: HLIT’s backlog growth reflects sustained investment in broadband and video delivery networks.

## 5. Questions For Follow-Up
- How sustainable is Ouster’s current lidar demand growth, especially beyond industrial/autonomy segments?  
- What are the margin outlook and cash flow drivers for Marvell given mixed earnings signals?  
- Are there emerging catalysts in Adeia’s IP licensing portfolio that could stabilize or accelerate revenue?  
- How will TSMC’s A14 AI process roadmap position the company versus competitors in next-gen AI chip supply?  
- What is the trajectory of CPS Technologies in defense-related advanced materials relative to broader market demand?
