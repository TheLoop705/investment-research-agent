# Daily Portfolio Digest

## 1. Executive Summary  
Ambarella (AMBA) and Super Micro Computer (SMCI) feature significant negative signals stemming from weaker guidance and external regulatory trade risks respectively. Conversely, Harmonic (HLIT), CPS Technologies (CPSH), Sivers Semiconductors (SIVE), and Marvell Technology (MRVL) report constructive earnings or product developments, offering potential growth signals. Ouster (OUST) shows record revenue with raised outlook, meriting further attention. Overall, semiconductors and AI infrastructure themes dominate recent developments with supply chain and regulatory risks emerging as key challenges.

- **Market Sentiment:** AI sector volatility pressuring names like Penguin despite fundamental collaborations.  
- **Guidance Uncertainty:** Ambarella and ServiceNow display cautious or mixed outlooks, warranting close monitoring.

- **Photonic & Optical Growth:** Sivers Semiconductors and CPS Technologies exhibit strong order books and strategic expansions in specialized components underpinning future growth.
