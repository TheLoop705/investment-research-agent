Several portfolio companies reported earnings with generally constructive outcomes, led by notable revenue beats and positive outlooks from Ouster (OUST), Navitas Semiconductor (NVTS), Super Micro Computer (SMCI), and Harmonic (HLIT). ## 4. Cross-Holding Themes

- **AI & Data Center Demand Drives Growth:** SMCI, NVTS, PENG, and to some extent OUST and VICR continue to benefit from trends in AI infrastructure and edge/data center acceleration. Product launches and strong bookings underline sustained market demand.  
- **Semiconductor Earning Divergence:** While SIVE and NVTS report strong order pipelines and bookings, Adeia’s licensing business struggles, highlighting sector and sub-industry performance differentiation.  
- **Power and Connectivity Infrastructure Strength:** AMSC, VICR, and HLIT reveal continued investment in grid, power modules, and video/broadband infrastructure respectively, reflecting broad market upgrade cycles.  
- **Product Innovation as Growth Lever:** Penguin and Sivers lead in new product announcements aligned with AI/data center and photonics growth themes. This underlines execution importance alongside pure financials.
