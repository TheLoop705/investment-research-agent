# Daily Portfolio Digest

## 1. Executive Summary  
Key portfolio companies reported earnings and strategic partnerships that shape near-term outlooks across semiconductors, photonics, AI infrastructure, and energy systems. Notable positive signals include record revenues at CPSH and AMSC, revenue growth optimism at LPK, and product/market expansion for OUST and NOW. Ambarella (AMBA) posted mixed earnings, highlighting automotive strength but balanced overall. Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing (TSM) remains in focus amid geopolitical chip export curbs. Sivers Semiconductors (SIVE) signals strategic growth.

## 3. Risk Flags  
- CPSH’s AT-market equity raising ($9.6M) could impact share supply dynamics and valuation.  
- AMSC’s strong revenue came with an earnings miss and cautious forward guidance, posing some valuation risk.  
- Ambarella’s mixed earnings and narrowly beating estimates call for careful monitoring of non-automotive segments.  
- Geopolitical tensions increase risks for TSM’s China supply chain exposure.  
- No immediate red flags flagged for other portfolio holdings, but continued monitoring warranted.
