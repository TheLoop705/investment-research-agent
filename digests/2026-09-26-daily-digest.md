# Daily Portfolio Digest

## 1. Executive Summary
Several portfolio companies reported earnings with mixed to constructive results, highlighting key revenue growth, raised guidance, and strategic partnerships that could influence their medium-term trajectory. Notably, Amprius Technologies (AMPX) delivered record revenues with raised guidance, while Ambarella (AMBA) posted modest revenue growth alongside long-term channel agreements. Ouster (OUST) advanced partnerships expanding lidar application footprints. Some companies like American Superconductor (AMSC) showed strong fundamentals but faced unclear market reactions. Follow-up on execution on strategic partnerships and margin dynamics is recommended.
