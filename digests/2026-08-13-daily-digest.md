# Daily Portfolio Digest - 2026-08-04

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Several portfolio companies released quarterly earnings, revealing mixed yet mostly constructive signals. Key positives came from CPS Technologies, Harmonic, Amprius, and Vicor reporting strong revenue growth or record backlogs. Ouster posted mixed results despite a large Utah lidar contract expansion, leading to stock weakness. Ambarella’s near-term outlook is uncertain amid takeover rumors. American Superconductor missed earnings estimates, highlighting margin pressure despite strong order intake. Other holdings like Infineon and Applied Optoelectronics posted notable growth tied to AI and photonics demand. Overall, the earnings season emphasizes growth in broadband, lidar, energy storage, and power semiconductors, tempered by margin challenges and cautious near-term guidance in some cases.
