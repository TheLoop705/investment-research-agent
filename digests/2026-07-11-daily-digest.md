- **Navitas Semiconductor (NVTS):** Exposure to patent infringement suit by Wolfspeed creates downside risk pending legal outcome.
- **CPS Technologies (CPSH):** Recent $9.6M at-the-market raise may dilute equity; watch for capital deployment effectiveness.
- **LPKF Laser & Electronics (LPK):** Profitability concerns persist despite revenue growth; operational efficiency needs improvement.
- **American Superconductor (AMSC):** Despite strong results, share price remains volatile (~-17% post-earnings), indicating possible market skepticism or liquidity pressures.
- **Sivers Semiconductors (SIVE):** Pipeline backed by CHIPS Act funds is under regulatory scrutiny, which may affect future revenue realization.
