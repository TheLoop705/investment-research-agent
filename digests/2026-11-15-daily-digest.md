## 4. Cross-Holding Themes

- **AI Demand Driving Optics and Semiconductor Growth**: Ouster (lidar), Sivers (wireless semiconductors for LiDAR), AAOI (optical networking), Marvell, and Penguin Solutions are benefiting from AI data center and automotive sensor demand surges.

- **Record Revenues and Raised Guidance**: Several portfolio names (OUST, CPSH, HLIT, AAOI) posted strong top-line results, implying robust sector-level demand in sensing, power solutions, and video infrastructure.

- **Valuation Stress in Chip Sector**: Despite growth, valuation debates persist notably around MRVL, linked to market volatility and yield environment, underscoring risk management needs.

- **Enterprise AI Partnerships and Product Innovation**: ServiceNow’s AI CRM partnership and Penguin’s CXL memory module reflect increased AI-driven innovation focus in software and infrastructure hardware.
