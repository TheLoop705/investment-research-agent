## 3. Risk Flags

- **OUST**: Despite contract wins, mixed Q3 revenue outlook creates short-term uncertainty.
- **AMSC**: Margin pressure and earnings miss highlight risks around cost control and profitability.
- **ADEA**: Mixed guidance introduces ambiguity in growth prospects in a niche IP licensing business.
- **AMBA**: Acquisition speculation adds volatility—pending regulatory and deal risk.
- **General**: Earnings season exposes vulnerability to evolving supply chain constraints and macroeconomic pressures impacting industrial and semiconductor demand.

- **M&A Activity**: Ambarella’s acquisition talks exemplify consolidation in automotive vision and edge AI chip markets.
