# Daily Portfolio Digest

## 1. Executive Summary
Several portfolio companies reported Q2 2026 earnings, delivering predominantly constructive results with notable revenue growth, record backlogs, and expanded opportunity pipelines. Key highlights include Amprius Technologies (AMPX) posting record revenue with raised guidance, Sivers Semiconductors (SIVE) showing a 268% pipeline surge, CPS Technologies (CPSH) and Harmonic (HLIT) reporting record revenues and raised outlooks. Marvell Technology (MRVL) presented mixed earnings with a stock decline despite strong AI bookings. Ouster (OUST) advances in lidar shipments and is repositioning strategically in autonomy. Emerging guidance and industry outlook updates for ServiceNow (NOW) suggest a mixed sentiment. There were no immediate risk flags but attention is warranted on margin guidance and market responses in some cases.

## 3. Risk Flags
- **Marvell Technology**: Stock price decline post-earnings raises concern on market reception of margin guidance despite strong AI revenue.  
- No explicit risk events reported for other holdings but monitoring upcoming releases, execution on raised guidance, and broader macro effects remains prudent.

## 4. Cross-Holding Themes
- **AI and Data Center**: Marvell, ServiceNow, and Ouster’s developments underscore strong AI demand across semiconductors, infrastructure software, and autonomy hardware.  
- **Growth in Specialized Semiconductors and Photonics**: Sivers and CPS Technologies’ record pipelines and revenues highlight robust demand across wireless, optics, and advanced materials.  
- **Energy and Battery Innovation**: Amprius demonstrates strong end-market traction in high-density battery solutions supporting drone and aviation applications.  
- **Broadband and Networking Expansion**: Harmonic’s backlog and broadband growth reaffirm the pivot toward broadband infrastructure in communication networks.

## 5. Questions For Follow-Up
- How sustainable is the rapid pipeline growth reported by Sivers Semiconductors, and what are the specific customer verticals driving this?  
- What are the margin outlook drivers and potential risks behind Marvell’s weaker-than-expected gross margin guidance?  
- Can Ouster maintain its 70% shipment growth in lidar under evolving competitive and supply chain conditions?  
- How might ServiceNow’s mixed guidance impact cadence for AI automation investments by enterprise clients?  
- Will Amprius’s higher drone demand extend to other commercial or defense battery markets?
