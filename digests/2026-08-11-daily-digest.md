- **AI Infrastructure and Semiconductors**: Strong reported growth for AI-related segments (IFX, SMCI, OUST, AMPX) reflecting robust demand across compute, sensors, and power supply chains.  
- **Power and Energy Management**: CPSH, VICR, and AMPX show positive developments indicating underlying expansion in high-performance and energy-efficient hardware.
