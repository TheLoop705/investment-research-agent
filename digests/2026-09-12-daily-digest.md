# Daily Portfolio Digest

Several portfolio companies reported earnings or notable partnership developments, with key highlights around growth and strategic alignment in AI, semiconductor, and energy sectors. ServiceNow (NOW) stands out with regulatory scrutiny focused on its AI governance tool. Ambarella (AMBA), Amprius Technologies (AMPX), Adeia (ADEA), CPS Technologies (CPSH), Harmonic (HLIT), Penguin Solutions (PENG), and Ouster (OUST) posted earnings or partnership news reinforcing growth outlooks or market positioning. Monitoring margin trends, sustained demand, and regulatory impact remains critical.

### ServiceNow (NOW)
- Regulatory spotlight on the "ServiceNow AI Control Tower" aimed at securing AI governance and improving enterprise ROI.
- Partnership catalyst: Fortune to hold inaugural Fortune AIQ Summit in partnership with ServiceNow.
- Update signals cautious regulatory environment but also proactive product positioning for AI governance.
  
### Ambarella (AMBA)
- Q2 revenue grew by 13.2%; signed long-duration (7-year) channel agreements.
- Earnings and revenues beat estimates but margin pressure noted.
- Core question is whether scalability of edge-AI chips can reverse margin compression trends.
  
### Amprius Technologies (AMPX)
- Reported record Q2 revenue, raised guidance; drone demand cited as a growth driver.
- Earnings call highlights a positive outlook for high-energy density battery tech.

### Adeia (ADEA)
- Mixed Q2 earnings; revenue reached $96M.
- Entered a multi-year IP license agreement with RPX.
- Earnings and IP licensing reinforce chronic growth but with some execution variability.

### CPS Technologies (CPSH)
- Record Q2 revenue and strategic wins highlighted.
- Secured initial defense order for tungsten components.
- Earnings suggest growth momentum in advanced materials with defense sector expansion.

### Harmonic (HLIT)
- Earnings beat and raised guidance led to a constructive sentiment shift.
- Record backlog and 54% broadband service revenue growth flagged.
- Positioning as a pure-play broadband video infrastructure company strengthens.

### Penguin Solutions (PENG)
- Launched initiatives to tackle AI data center power challenges.
- Full-stack AI factory platform supports distributed AI data centers.
- Market response positive amid AI infrastructure demand surge.

### Ouster (OUST)
- Partnership with Flyability showcased for industrial lidar applications.
- Q2 lidar shipments surged 70% YoY.
- YTD stock up 70%, demonstrating strong market confidence in growth trajectory.
