# Daily Portfolio Digest – June 2024

---

## 1. Executive Summary  
Key earnings and guidance updates across portfolio names showed constructive top-line growth and select margin improvements amid mixed outlooks. Notable momentum drivers are Ambarella’s edge-AI revenue gain and TSMC’s raised outlook on AI-related chip demand. Several semiconductor and energy infrastructure names reported record or strong revenue, signaling expanding end-market demand, though margin pressures and sustainability of growth remain questions for some. Lidar leader Ouster demonstrated significant shipment growth, but sustainability of demand is yet to be clarified.

---

## 2. Highest-Signal Portfolio Updates

**Ambarella (AMBA)**  
- Q2 revenue grew 13.2% YoY, surpassing estimates.  
- Signed 7-year channel agreements supporting longer-term revenue visibility.  
- Mixed earnings sentiment reflects ongoing margin compression in edge AI, with potential for scaling to reverse this trend.  
- [Source](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/ambarella-amba-grew-2q-revenue-231537538.html?.tsrc=rss)

**Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing (TSM)**  
- Raised forward guidance due to strong demand for advanced chips, driven by AI and data center workloads.  
- $265B US expansion part of broader semiconductor diplomacy strategy.  
- ASML selected TSMC over Samsung for new EUV lithography machines, indicating tech leadership.  
- [Source](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/taiwan-semiconductor-manufacturing-company-tsm-142602670.html?.tsrc=rss)

**Amprius Technologies (AMPX)**  
- Reported record Q2 revenue with raised guidance, fueled by increasing drone market demand.  
- Indicates growing commercial adoption of high-energy-density battery technology.  
- [Source](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/amprius-technologies-inc-ampx-q2-210251235.html?.tsrc=rss)

**Adeia (ADEA)**  
- Q2 revenue hit $96M, though market reception mixed.  
- Secured multi-year IP license agreement with RPX, enhancing patent monetization outlook.  
- [Source](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/adeia-inc-adea-q2-2026-050634400.html?.tsrc=rss)

**CPS Technologies (CPSH)**  
- Posted record Q2 revenue and achieved strategic milestones in tungsten components for defense.  
- Signed initial defense contract for thermal management materials, signaling new growth avenues.  
- [Source](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/cps-technologies-corp-cpsh-q2-010513160.html?.tsrc=rss)

**American Superconductor (AMSC)**  
- Mixed Q2 earnings outcomes: beat expectations but share price declined on unclear market factors.  
- Strong Q1 momentum and profitability guidance noted, indicating evolving bull case.  
- [Source](https://finance.yahoo.com/energy/articles/q2-earnings-roundup-american-superconductor-013223459.html?.tsrc=rss)

**Harmonic (HLIT)**  
- Delivered Q2 earnings beat with raised guidance.  
- Record backlog and 54% broadband revenue growth highlighted; pure-play broadband pivot continuing.  
- [Source](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/harmonic-hlit-cheap-earnings-beat-161222966.html?.tsrc=rss)

**Ouster (OUST)**  
- LiDAR shipment volume jumped 70% YoY in Q2.  
- Partnership with GeoCue and repositioning at John Deere’s autonomy platform are key signals of growth potential.  
- Sustainability of demand post initial ramp remains uncertain.  
- [Source](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/articles/ousters-lidar-shipments-jump-70-160000872.html?.tsrc=rss)

---

## 3. Risk Flags  
- Ambarella faces ongoing margin compression in edge AI despite revenue growth; careful monitoring advised.  
- American Superconductor’s share price reaction suggests possible investor concerns despite operational beats.  
- Ouster’s strong shipment growth questions sustainability; market demand volatility potential.  
- Adeia’s mixed earnings market response implies patent monetization may face hurdles or uncertainties.

---

## 4. Cross-Holding Themes  
- **Semiconductor Growth Fueled by AI**: TSMC, Ambarella, Marvell, Infineon, and others see demand driven by AI compute and edge processing, but scaling economics and supply chain investments (like TSMC’s US expansion) are critical.  
- **Energy & Infrastructure Momentum**: Amprius (battery tech) and American Superconductor (grid systems) reflect rising focus on energy transition, with CPS Technologies expanding defense thermal materials.  
- **Optical and Photonics Enablers**: Sivers and Ouster highlights demonstrate growth in lidar and photonics, key for autonomy and advanced sensing markets.  
- **Enterprise Software & AI Automation**: ServiceNow (NOW) remains a thematic holding for workflow automation but lacks dominant fresh signals today.

---

## 5. Questions For Follow-Up  
- Can Ambarella’s channel agreements and scaling reverse current edge AI margin pressures sustainably?  
- What is the anticipated timeline and impact of TSMC’s US capacity expansion on global supply dynamics and geopolitics?  
- How durable is Ouster’s lidar shipment growth beyond partnerships with GeoCue and John Deere?  
- Are Adeia’s IP licensing trajectories indicative of a longer-term stable revenue base or episodic?  
- What market or fundamental triggers caused AMSC’s negative share price reaction despite earnings beats?
