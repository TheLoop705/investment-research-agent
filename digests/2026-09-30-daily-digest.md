# Daily Portfolio Digest

## Executive Summary

The spotlight today is on earnings reports and product developments from multiple portfolio companies, with several showing constructive trends in revenues, bookings, and strategic partnerships. Amprius Technologies (AMPX) reported record revenues and raised guidance bolstered by defense grants and drone demand, while Ambarella’s (AMBA) 2Q revenue grew 13.2%, supported by long-term channel agreements despite margin pressures. Sivers Semiconductors (SIVE) posted a significant 268% jump in its sales pipeline, positioning it for growth acceleration. Marvell Technology (MRVL) has begun shipments of custom AI chips and sees higher forecasts ahead. ServiceNow (NOW) faces rising regulatory concerns regarding AI platform security, challenging its valuation on AI hype. Overall, semiconductor and AI infrastructure holdings reveal momentum but with mixed signals on valuation and competition.
