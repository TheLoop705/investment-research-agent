# Daily Portfolio Digest – June 2024

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Multiple portfolio companies reported or pre-announced Q2 2026 earnings with generally constructive results, complemented by some strategic partnership news. Noteworthy earnings beats and raised guidance emerged from Amprius Technologies (AMPX), CPS Technologies (CPSH), Harmonic (HLIT), and Sivers Semiconductors (SIVE), supporting growth narratives in energy storage, advanced materials, broadband infrastructure, and photonics/wireless semiconductors respectively. Ambarella (AMBA) showed moderate revenue growth and secured long-term channel agreements, indicating steady traction amid margin pressure. American Superconductor (AMSC) delivered mixed earnings results with strong Q1 momentum noted but stock reaction weak. ServiceNow (NOW) advanced AI-driven enterprise workflow positioning through notable partnerships and raised outlooks.

## 3. Risk Flags

- Ambarella faces ongoing margin compression risks despite revenue growth; sustainability of scale gains in edge AI markets requires monitoring.  
- American Superconductor’s mixed earnings and weak stock response suggest possible market doubts on renewable segment momentum or execution risks.  
- Adeia (ADEA) earnings coverage is mixed, signaling potential volatility or uncertainty in IP licensing business momentum.  
- No explicit new risks reported for other holdings but close watch on semiconductor industry cyclicality remains relevant.
