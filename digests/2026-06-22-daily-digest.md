## 3. Risk Flags

- **CPSH**: The $9.6M at-the-market stock offering may dilute shares and reflects capital raising needs.  
- **AMSC**: Earnings miss and cautious guidance raise questions about near-term growth sustainability despite strong revenue gains.  
- **SMCI**: Large $7B capital raise suggests increasing leverage and execution risk in scaling AI server deployments.  
- No immediate risks reported for the other holdings with recent signals.

---

## 4. Cross-Holding Themes

- **AI & Edge Computing Growth**: OUST’s lidar expansions, AMBA’s automotive AI chip strength, SMCI’s AI server capital raise, and NOW’s AI workflow partnerships collectively highlight intensified investments across sensing, processing, infrastructure, and software layers in AI.  
- **Semiconductor Manufacturing and Photonics**: SIVE’s strategic pipeline and CHIPS Act involvement, LPK’s growing laser revenues, and Ambarella’s solid semiconductor earnings underscore sustained momentum in chips, photonics, and advanced packaging.  
- **Energy & Infrastructure Transition**: AMSC’s mixed but substantial earnings growth along with CPSH’s record revenues reinforce ongoing shifts in grid and power system modernization.  
- **Capital Requirements & Funding**: SMCI and CPSH signal funding needs (large capital raise and stock offering respectively), pointing to operational scaling or liquidity strategies in growth segments.
