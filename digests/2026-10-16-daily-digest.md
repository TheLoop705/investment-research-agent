# Daily Portfolio Digest

## 1. Executive Summary
Several portfolio companies reported earnings or announced key partnerships, revealing mixed to constructive signals across sectors such as AI infrastructure, semiconductor supply chain, photonics, and energy storage. Highlights include record revenues and raised guidance at Amprius Technologies (AMPX) and strong pipeline growth for Sivers Semiconductors (SIVE). ServiceNow (NOW) remains in focus amid its AI platform rollout and competitive revenue trends versus Salesforce, while Ambarella (AMBA) shows revenue growth offset by margin pressures, raising strategic questions on scaling. Ouster (OUST) is advancing in drone lidar via partnerships, potentially diversifying its autonomy strategy.

## 3. Risk Flags
- Ambarella (AMBA) faces ongoing margin compression risks despite revenue growth in edge AI.
- Ouster (OUST) execution risk in extending lidar applications via drone partnerships remains to be validated by Q3 results.
- While no explicit near-term risks flagged for Amprius (AMPX), dependency on defense contracts can introduce volatility.
- Adeia (ADEA) showed mixed earnings, warranting monitoring of IP licensing business stability.
