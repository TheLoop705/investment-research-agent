# Daily Portfolio Digest

## 1. Executive Summary
Several portfolio companies reported earnings with constructive revenue growth, notably in advanced technology and energy sectors. Key highlights include record revenues from Ouster (OUST) in lidar, Harmonic (HLIT) in broadband/video delivery, CPS Technologies (CPSH) in advanced materials, and American Superconductor (AMSC) in grid systems.
