# Daily Portfolio Digest

## 1. Executive Summary
Recent earnings season delivers a mix of growth signals and strategic shifts across key portfolio holdings, especially in semiconductor, photonics, and energy infrastructure themes. Ambarella (AMBA) and Amprius Technologies (AMPX) report strong revenue growth, while Ouster (OUST) shows rapid shipment increases in lidar. CPS Technologies (CPSH) and Harmonic (HLIT) highlight record revenues and backlog strength, indicating positive demand dynamics in advanced materials and broadband sectors. Conversely, American Superconductor (AMSC) exhibits mixed investor reaction despite beating earnings and raising guidance. Multiple companies announce longer-term agreements and strategic partnerships that may influence future growth trajectories. Semiconductor packaging and foundry names like ASE and Infineon (IFX) reveal strong AI- and power-related demand signals.
