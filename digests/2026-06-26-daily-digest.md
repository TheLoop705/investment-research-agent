Key earnings reports from Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing (TSM), CPS Technologies (CPSH), LPKF Laser (LPK), American Superconductor (AMSC), Ambarella (AMBA), and Sivers Semiconductors (SIVE) shaped the day’s updates.
