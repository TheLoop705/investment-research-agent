## 1. Executive Summary

Several portfolio holdings reported Q2 2026 earnings with mostly constructive outcomes, highlighting strong revenue growth, backlog increases, and strategic market positioning particularly in broadband, energy storage, power modules, and optical components. Notably, Ouster reported a sharp 70% Y/Y increase in lidar shipments but faces mixed revenue outlooks and stock pressure after some contract wins. Marvell deepened its role in AI data center co-packaged optics (CPO), reinforcing exposure to AI infrastructure. ServiceNow’s substantial $7.75B AI security spend introduces regulatory scrutiny risks. Adeia and Ouster earnings were more mixed, warranting selective monitoring.  

## 2. Highest-Signal Portfolio Updates

- **CPSH (CPS Technologies)**: Record revenue reported for Q2 2026, with strategic initiatives driving growth. Earnings call tone highly constructive.  
- **OUST (Ouster)**: LiDAR shipments jumped 70% Y/Y in Q2, indicating strong demand growth; however, mixed Q3 revenue outlook and a 5.7% stock decline post Utah lidar contract create questions on sustaining momentum.  
- **HLIT (Harmonic)**: Q2 earnings beat with record backlog and 54% broadband segment growth; raised guidance supports bullish broadband infrastructure pivot.  
- **AMPX (Amprius Technologies)**: Record revenue and higher guidance, with notable demand from drone applications signaling expanding energy storage relevance.  
- **ADEA (Adeia)**: Q2 revenue reached $96M, but earnings view mixed; noteworthy is a new multi-year IP license agreement with RPX, reflecting strategic licensing traction.  
- **MRVL (Marvell Technology)**: Deepening of co-packaged optics role in AI data center buildouts enhances AI infrastructure exposure; product-market strategy looks strong.  
- **VICR (Vicor)**: Strong revenue growth and backlog more than doubled despite stock price softness; earnings call constructive on strategic execution.  
- **NOW (ServiceNow)**: Focused on AI security with planned $7.75B spend, introducing potential regulatory risk and cost pressure.  

## 3. Risk Flags

- **OUST**: Mixed Q3 revenue outlook and share price decline despite shipment growth create uncertainty about near-term demand sustainability.  
- **NOW**: Large AI security investment raises regulatory scrutiny concerns and potential margin implications.  
- **ADEA**: Mixed earnings signals despite revenue milestones warrant monitoring of profitability and licensing revenue stability.  
- **VICR**: Stock attenuated on valuation concerns despite backlog gains; potential disconnect between fundamentals and market sentiment.  

## 4. Cross-Holding Themes

- **AI/Data Center Infrastructure**: Marvell, Vicor, Supermicro, and Taiwan Semiconductor show strengthening positioning around AI compute hardware, co-packaged optics, power modules, and server infrastructure.  
- **Broadband/Networking**: Harmonic and Applied Optoelectronics reflect accelerating demand in broadband and optical networking components amid digital infrastructure buildouts.  
- **Energy & Power Systems**: CPS Technologies, American Superconductor, and Amprius highlight robust activity in energy storage, advanced materials, and grid technology reflecting green energy trends.  
- **Optics & Photonics**: Exposure through Sivers, M7U (Nynomic), and AAOI tied to optical communication and sensing markets shows mixed valuation and growth signals.  
- **Regulatory & Security**: ServiceNow's major AI security investment spotlights increasing regulatory and security imperatives in enterprise AI deployments.  

## 5. Questions For Follow-Up

- Can Ouster sustain its rapid lidar shipment growth and convert multimillion-dollar orders into solid revenue streams given the cautious Q3 outlook?  
- How will Vicor address share price valuation gaps despite strong backlog and growth signals?  
- What are the implications of ServiceNow’s $7.75B AI security expense on margins and regulatory exposure?  
- Does Adeia’s mixed earnings outlook impact the durability of its IP licensing growth trajectory?  
- How are Marvell’s expanded CPO engagements expected to drive revenue and market share in AI data center silicon over the next 12 months?
