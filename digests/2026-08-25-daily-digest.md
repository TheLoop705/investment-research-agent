# Daily Portfolio Digest

## 1. Executive Summary
Multiple portfolio companies reported earnings with mixed to constructive receptions, highlighted by strong revenue growth and expanded AI-related partnerships. ServiceNow extended a multiyear AI partnership demonstrating growing enterprise AI adoption. Amprius Technologies delivered record Q2 revenue and raised guidance, reflecting robust demand in energy storage. Ouster’s LiDAR shipments surged 70% YoY, though the stock faces revenue outlook concerns. Texas Instruments and Adeia showed mixed earnings signals, with some valuation and growth uncertainties. Power and semiconductor firms like CPS Technologies, Vicor, and Infineon posted encouraging results tied to strategic growth and AI revenue.

## 2. Highest-Signal Portfolio Updates

### ServiceNow (NOW)
- Expanded multiyear AI partnership with Tech Mahindra focusing on enterprise AI deployment at scale.
- Stock up 41.1% since last earnings, reflecting strong market confidence in AI-driven workflow automation.
- Partnership signals growing adoption of AI in enterprise software ecosystems.

### Amprius Technologies (AMPX)
- Q2 2026 earnings showcased record revenue and higher guidance driven by drone and energy storage demand.
- Positive earnings momentum suggests strengthening position in high-energy-density battery technology.

### Ouster (OUST)
- LiDAR shipments increased 70% YoY in Q2.
- Recent Utah traffic modernization contract awarded, but mixed revenue guidance led to a 5.7% stock decline.
- Key focus on sustaining momentum amid competitive lidar market.

### Texas Instruments (TXN)
- Q2 earnings report mixed; stock down 6.8% since earnings.
- Analyst target price increased, reflecting a fair value reassessment.
- Challenges persist around industrial cycle softness despite diverse analog semiconductor exposure.

### Adeia (ADEA)
- Q2 revenue reached $96M; earnings coverage mixed.
- Announced upcoming Q2 2026 financial results on Aug 3, plus a new multi-year IP licensing deal with RPX.
- IP licensing strategy remains a key driver amid variable financial results.

### CPS Technologies (CPSH)
- Posted record Q2 revenue and strategic growth initiatives.
- Earnings call highlighted expansion in advanced materials and thermal management markets.

### Vicor (VICR)
- Strong Q2 revenue growth and strategic progress in power module solutions.
- Positive outlook in compute and industrial power semiconductor demand.

### Infineon Technologies (IFX)
- Set record AI revenue, underscoring strength in power semiconductors and automotive chips aligned with AI and industrial electronics demand.
- Building on AI-driven growth opportunities.

### Harmonic (HLIT)
- Beat earnings and raised guidance.
- Record backlog with 54% growth in broadband.
- Positive signals for broadband/networking infrastructure demand.

## 3. Risk Flags
- Ouster’s mixed Q3 revenue outlook suggests possible volatility in lidar demand sustainability.
- Texas Instruments faces pressure from slowing industrial cycles despite valuation improvements.
- Adeia’s mixed earnings indicate uncertainty in IP licensing growth trajectory.
- American Superconductor (AMSC) stock fell despite beating Q2 expectations, signaling potential market skepticism despite profitability guidance.
- Super Micro Computer (SMCI) strong guidance contrasts legal challenges, a potential risk to monitor.

## 4. Cross-Holding Themes
- **AI & Enterprise Software:** ServiceNow and Infineon stand out for AI-related revenue and partnerships.
- **Semiconductors & Power:** Broad strength across CPSH, VICR, IFX, TXN, and NVTS in power and high-performance semiconductor markets.
- **Energy Storage & Infrastructure:** Amprius Technologies and American Superconductor highlight energy and grid infrastructure growth, with varied market reactions.
- **Optics and Photonics:** Ouster (lidar) and Sivers Semiconductors signal growth but question demand sustainability.
- **Networking & Data Center:** Harmonic’s broadband backlog and Vicor’s power modules underscore infrastructure build-outs tied to data and communications.
