## 3. Risk Flags  

- **AMBA:** Margin compression risk persists despite revenue growth; edge AI market remains competitive and capital-intensive.  
- **NOW:** Elevated valuation multiples (80x earnings) ahead of earnings raise execution risk for AI automation growth and integration of security verticals.  
- **OUST:** Perception of overvaluation persists despite strategic partnership progress; reliance on defense and autonomy market adoption adds cyclicality.  
- **AMSC:** Renewable energy segment’s mixed results and macro uncertainties continue to pressure stock performance.  
- **VICR:** Uncertainty over AI royalty frameworks could impact future revenue streams and profitability.
