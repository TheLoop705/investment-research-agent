## 3. Risk Flags
- **TSM**: Valuation considered high relative to earnings; market may be pricing in aggressive AI-driven growth that could be delayed.
- **OUST**: Despite contract wins, stock price weakness indicates skepticism on revenue outlook and sector competition.
- **AAOI**: Potential overvaluation (up to 93%) despite optimism from new customer deals; risk of downside if execution falters.
- **Broad Sector**: Semiconductor companies show varied earnings results with potential supply/demand imbalances and increasing competitive pressures.
- No direct risks reported for CPSH, HLIT, AMPX, or ADEA.
