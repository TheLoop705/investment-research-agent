## 1. Executive Summary

Key developments today center on strong earnings results and partnership announcements across several portfolio companies, notably in lidar/autonomy, AI infrastructure, and semiconductor manufacturing. Ouster (OUST) is advancing its autonomy strategy through drone lidar partnerships, while Amprius Technologies (AMPX) and Penguin Solutions (PENG) delivered record revenues and raised guidance. ## 3. Risk Flags

- **ServiceNow (NOW)** faces increased AI-related competitive litigation (Edwin AI investigations), which could impact product adoption and customer confidence.  
- **Ambarella (AMBA)** margin compression risk persists despite revenue growth; ability to scale edge AI chips profitably remains uncertain.  
- **Amprius Technologies (AMPX)** reliance on defense grants and drone demand poses concentration risk.  
- No explicit risks noted for Ouster or other earnings-positive companies, but sector cyclicality and supply chain remain general watchpoints.

- **AI and Edge Computing Growth**: Multiple holdings (AMBA, PENG, NOW) advancing AI-enabling products; partnerships and long-term contracts critical to scaling business.
