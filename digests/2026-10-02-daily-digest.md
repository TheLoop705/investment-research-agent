# Daily Portfolio Digest

## 1. Executive Summary
Several portfolio companies reported Q2 2026 earnings or corporate updates with generally constructive outcomes, highlighted by record revenues, pipeline growth, and strategic deals. Amprius Technologies (AMPX) stands out with record revenues and raised guidance amid strong defense grant news. Ambarella (AMBA) showed solid revenue growth with long-term channel agreements but faces margin pressures. Semiconductor-related firms like Sivers Semiconductors (SIVE) and CPS Technologies (CPSH) also posted positive earnings and pipeline expansions. ServiceNow (NOW) presents caution amid concerns around AI security pricing risk. Networking player Harmonic (HLIT) beat expectations and raised guidance, signaling strengthening fundamentals. Some industrial and semiconductor firms (e.g., American Superconductor, Texas Instruments) showed mixed to cautious sentiments requiring monitoring.
