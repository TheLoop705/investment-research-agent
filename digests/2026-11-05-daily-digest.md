# Daily Portfolio Digest

## 1. Executive Summary  
Several portfolio companies reported recent earnings, showing mostly constructive revenue growth signals and raised outlooks, especially in AI-related and semiconductor segments. Highlights include strong quarterly results from Ouster (OUST), Harmonic (HLIT), Navitas Semiconductor (NVTS), and ServiceNow (NOW) with emphasis on AI and infrastructure expansion. By contrast, Super Micro Computer (SMCI) and Adeia (ADEA) showed caution due to cash flow concerns and earnings misses. American Superconductor (AMSC) posted solid results but with mixed sentiment on growth durability. CPS Technologies (CPSH) stood out with record revenue and strategic progress in defense orders. Follow-ups should monitor margin trends and guidance revisions given mixed signals on cash and growth.
