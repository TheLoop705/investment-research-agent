# Daily Portfolio Digest

## 1. Executive Summary
Today’s top developments center on portfolio companies reporting Q2 2026 earnings with generally constructive outlooks and operational updates. Notable highlights include Amprius Technologies’ record revenues and raised guidance amid expanded defense funding, Ambarella’s revenue growth paired with strategic channel agreements despite margin pressure, and Taiwanese TSMC’s advancing discussions on a US chipmaking partnership to bolster supply-chain resilience. These signal ongoing momentum in semiconductors, AI infrastructure, photonics, and energy materials sectors, with some margin and competitive dynamics meriting closer attention.
