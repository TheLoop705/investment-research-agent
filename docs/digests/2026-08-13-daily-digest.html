1. Executive Summary

Several portfolio companies released quarterly earnings, revealing mixed yet mostly constructive signals. Key positives came from CPS Technologies, Harmonic, Amprius, and Vicor reporting strong revenue growth or record backlogs. Ouster posted mixed results despite a large Utah lidar contract expansion, leading to stock weakness. Ambarella’s near-term outlook is uncertain amid takeover rumors.
