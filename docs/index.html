1. Executive Summary

Several portfolio companies reported Q2 2026 earnings with predominantly constructive signals, highlighting record revenues, pipeline growth, and positive strategic developments. Notable earnings beats came from Amprius Technologies (AMPX), CPS Technologies (CPSH), Harmonic (HLIT), and Sivers Semiconductors (SIVE). Penguin Solutions (PENG) experienced market reaction to AI demand concerns.

Taiwan Semiconductor (TSM) remains in focus due to valuation concerns despite a 2nm process win, and Marvell Technology (MRVL) is deepening its AI infrastructure positioning. The broad theme of AI and infrastructure impacts earnings expectations and stock reactions across several holdings.
