Daily Portfolio Digest - 2026-08-04

1. Executive Summary

Several portfolio companies released quarterly earnings, revealing mixed yet mostly constructive signals. Key positives came from CPS Technologies, Harmonic, Amprius, and Vicor reporting strong revenue growth or record backlogs. Ouster posted mixed results despite a large Utah lidar contract expansion, leading to stock weakness. Ambarella’s near-term outlook is uncertain amid takeover rumors. American Superconductor missed earnings estimates, highlighting margin pressure despite strong order intake. Other holdings like Infineon and Applied Optoelectronics posted notable growth tied to AI and photonics demand. Overall, the earnings season emphasizes growth in broadband, lidar, energy storage, and power semiconductors, tempered by margin challenges and cautious near-term guidance in some cases.

2. Highest-Signal Portfolio Updates

CPS Technologies (CPSH)

  • Reported record revenue for Q2 2026, signaling strong execution in advanced materials and thermal management.
  • Featured strategic updates and high relevance to portfolio outlook.
  • Interpretation: Momentum in advanced materials likely supports medium-term demand in defense and thermal applications.
  • Source

Harmonic (HLIT)

  • Q2 earnings and revenues beat estimates with a 54% growth in broadband business backlog.
  • Record backlog points to sustained demand for broadband and video delivery infrastructure.
  • Interpretation: Positive validation of ongoing adoption in video delivery tech despite competitive pressures.
  • Source

Amprius Technologies (AMPX)

  • Delivered record revenue in Q2 and raised forward guidance, driven by higher drone demand.
  • Earnings call highlighted elevated energy-storage traction.
  • Interpretation: Strength in battery and aviation energy density critical for emerging drone applications.
  • Source

Vicor (VICR)

  • Posted strong Q2 revenue growth with strategic partnerships underlining power module demand in compute and industrial markets.
  • Some valuation discrepancies noted between cash flow and earnings metrics.
  • Interpretation: Growth should continue with potential re-rating as cash flow visibility improves.
  • Source

Ouster (OUST)

  • Q2 earnings beat on sensor growth but stock fell 5.7% after mixed Q3 revenue outlook.
  • Awarded multimillion-dollar Utah BlueCity lidar contract expansion covering 160 intersections.
  • Interpretation: Large contract wins showcase commercial traction; near-term caution on mixed guidance explains price weakness.
