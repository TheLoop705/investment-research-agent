Several portfolio companies reported recent earnings with constructive themes highlighted by record or beat-topline results and raised outlooks in key sectors: optical networking (AAOI), lidar/perception hardware (OUST), photonics/wireless semiconductors (SIVE), advanced materials (CPSH), battery technology (AMPX), and broadband/video infrastructure (HLIT).
