Daily Portfolio Digest – Selected Updates for 2026-09-11

1. Executive Summary

Key portfolio developments today emphasize earnings seasons, strategic partnerships, and regulatory environment developments in AI and semiconductor hardware. Of particular note are robust quarterly revenues and raised guidance at Amprius Technologies (AMPX) and CPS Technologies (CPSH), sustained sales growth and channel agreements at Ambarella (AMBA), and regulatory caution tempered by AI governance initiatives at ServiceNow (NOW). Ouster’s (OUST) expanding lidar shipment momentum and its partnering with Flyability highlights growing industrial application traction. Meanwhile, Taiwan Semiconductor (TSM) shows continued robust AI chip demand fueling a sharp revenue jump.

Partnerships & Product Initiatives

  • Ouster (OUST): Showcased lidar partnership with Flyability at INTERGEO 2026 focusing on advanced industrial inspection applications; shipment volume up 70% YoY.
  • Penguin Solutions (PENG): Launched AI data center power solutions aiming at full-stack AI infrastructure, supporting distributed AI workloads.
  • ServiceNow (NOW): Collaboration with Fortune for AIQ Summit; AI Control Tower targets enterprise security/governance, with regulatory concerns noted.
  • Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing (TSM): Reported 53% revenue increase amidst surging AI chip demand, highlighting AI compute supply chain strength.

3. Risk Flags

  • Ambarella (AMBA): Margin compression risks persist despite top-line growth and channel agreements.

  • Defense and industrial exposure: CPSH’s strategic defense orders and materials innovations align with broader industrial and national security sector interest.
