  • ServiceNow (NOW) had a mixed Q2 earnings roundup, spotlighting automation software segment uncertainties.

Partnership Development

Notable Growth Signals

  • Texas Instruments (TXN) maintained premium valuation signals linked to earnings strength, analog leadership, and industrial cycle exposure.
