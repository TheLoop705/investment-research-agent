Daily Portfolio Digest – August 19, 2026

1. Executive Summary

Key earnings reports and event-driven updates dominated a busy day for the portfolio, with several companies signaling strong growth in advanced technology segments such as AI, power systems, and photonics, alongside mixed signals in lidar and semiconductor licensing.

Noteworthy highlights include record revenue and backlog expansions at CPS Technologies, Harmonic, Amprius Technologies, and Vicor, with Infineon setting new AI revenue records. Ouster showed significant shipment growth but faced a negative market reaction amid cautious outlook commentary.

ServiceNow’s substantial AI security investment points to a strategic push into enterprise AI regulation and compliance, raising questions around future growth trajectories. Adeia posted solid revenue versus mixed interpretations.

Harmonic (HLIT)

ServiceNow (NOW)

Amprius Technologies (AMPX)

Adeia (ADEA)

Vicor (VICR)

Infineon Technologies (IFX)
