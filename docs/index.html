Key earnings and product developments emerged today across the portfolio, with notable mixed earnings reactions but largely constructive operational trends. Marvell (MRVL), American Superconductor (AMSC), Taiwan Semiconductor (TSM), Amprius Technologies (AMPX), Adeia (ADEA), CPS Technologies (CPSH), and ServiceNow (NOW) reported financial results or product news.
