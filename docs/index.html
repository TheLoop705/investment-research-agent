1. Executive Summary

Today’s most salient developments come from earnings reports and strategic partnerships within the portfolio. Notable earnings beats and raised outlooks were reported by Penguin Solutions (PENG), Amprius Technologies (AMPX), CPS Technologies (CPSH), Harmonic (HLIT), and Sivers Semiconductors (SIVE). Ambarella (AMBA) and Adeia (ADEA) delivered mixed earnings impressions. ServiceNow (NOW) maintains high relevance with ongoing AI-driven revenue discussions. Ouster (OUST) advances its autonomy strategy through drone lidar partnerships. Texas Instruments (TXN) continues to attract attention for potential shifts in the industrial and AI chip investment narrative due to U.S. capacity expansion.

  • Source: Yahoo Finance
