1. Executive Summary

Several portfolio companies reported recent earnings, showing mostly constructive revenue growth signals and raised outlooks, especially in AI-related and semiconductor segments. Highlights include strong quarterly results from Ouster (OUST), Harmonic (HLIT), Navitas Semiconductor (NVTS), and ServiceNow (NOW) with emphasis on AI and infrastructure expansion. By contrast, Super Micro Computer (SMCI) and Adeia (ADEA) showed caution due to cash flow concerns and earnings misses. Follow-ups should monitor margin trends and guidance revisions given mixed signals on cash and growth.
