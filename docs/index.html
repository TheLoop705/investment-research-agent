1. Executive Summary

Several portfolio companies recently reported earnings with predominantly constructive outlooks and growth signals, particularly in semiconductors, energy infrastructure, photonics, and AI-related sectors. Ambarella (AMBA) stands out for record automotive AI revenue driving a strong profit turnaround, while Vicor (VICR) showed robust revenue growth and backlog expansion. American Superconductor (AMSC) posted strong earnings but has seen share price volatility despite upbeat guidance. Applied Optoelectronics (AAOI) gained investor interest ahead of earnings amid optical transceiver expansion. Ouster (OUST), despite setting its Q2 call date, faces mixed sentiment due to share dilution fears. Follow-up is warranted on evolving AI impacts across holdings and on any emerging risks from capital raises or macro shifts.
