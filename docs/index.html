1. Executive Summary

Today’s key portfolio developments center largely on earnings reports across several holdings—Texas Instruments (TXN), Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing (TSM), CPS Technologies (CPSH), Amprius Technologies (AMPX), Adeia (ADEA), and American Superconductor (AMSC)—with mixed to constructive market receptions. ServiceNow (NOW) stands out on a major partnership announcement, supporting its recent strong price performance. Super Micro Computer (SMCI) faces legal scrutiny, weighing on its AI growth narrative despite positive operational signs. Ouster (OUST) registered solid shipment growth but saw a share pullback following mixed outlook commentary. Overall, semiconductor and AI-related segments continue to show revenue growth signals but face valuation debates and risk considerations.
