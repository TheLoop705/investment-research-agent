Several portfolio companies reported Q2 2026 earnings, revealing mixed-to-constructive signals across sectors like AI infrastructure, semiconductors, photonics, and energy systems. Overall, recent developments underscore evolving dynamics in AI, power, and broadband infrastructure segments.
