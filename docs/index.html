Daily Portfolio Digest – Semiconductor, AI, and Infrastructure Holdings

Date: 2026-11-16

1. Executive Summary

  • Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing (TSM) shares dropped over 4%, driven by concerns over a key customer’s reduced AI demand outlook and new U.S. regulatory checks on chip foundries. This adds uncertainty to the AI-driven growth narrative for the foundry sector.
  • CPS Technologies (CPSH) reported record Q2 revenue and secured initial defense orders for tungsten components, suggesting strong momentum in specialized materials and defense-related thermal management.
  • ServiceNow (NOW) showed ongoing sales strength and raised its AI contract value target to $1.7B, despite notable market value losses recently; AI adoption in workflow automation is accelerating.
  • Penguin Solutions (PENG) partnered with Marvell Technology (MRVL) to enhance data center AI memory infrastructure, expanding Penguin’s ComputeAI portfolio with NVIDIA rack-scale systems—validating its AI/HPC infrastructure pivot.
  • Harmonic (HLIT) posted record Q3 revenue with an improved outlook, showing resilient demand in video delivery and broadband infrastructure.
  • Ouster (OUST) secured a sizable second lidar traffic management contract in Nashville for 100 additional intersections, affirming its traction in smart city applications alongside record Q3 revenue.
  • Sivers Semiconductors (SIVE) reported a 268% pipeline increase, with new production orders from strategic lidar customers, supporting growth in wireless photonics and optical segments.
  • Marvell Technology (MRVL) gained despite broader market dips, buoyed by its AI data center product momentum and collaboration with Penguin Solutions.
  • Amprius Technologies (AMPX) beat Q3 revenue estimates but posted a quarterly loss, reflecting ongoing challenges in high-energy-density battery commercialization.
  • Adeia (ADEA) missed Q3 earnings and revenue expectations, indicating a potential slowdown in IP licensing revenues.
